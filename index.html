WHO WE ARE

Hope starts with a community that cares.

Tabitha Children's Outreach is a community-focused initiative based in Buwama, Mpigi District, Central Uganda. We are committed to helping vulnerable children and families access education, basic support and opportunities for a better future.

Our approach is simple: listen to community needs, mobilize responsible support, and turn that support into practical help that can make a lasting difference.

Our Mission

To transform the lives of vulnerable children, people and families through education, compassion and community support.

Our Vision

A community where every vulnerable child has the opportunity to learn, grow and build a brighter future.
